We picked several simple problems from European Championship 2025 and implemented their solutions in Kotlin, but there’s a mistake in the code. Your goal is to find this mistake and correct it. You should only make a small correction rather than rewrite the entire code.

Find one mistake and correct it (each task has exactly one mistake) to get a souvenir at the booth! Correct three mistakes and you get another souvenir :)

Task 2: [Ads](https://codeforces.com/contest/2068/problem/C)

Task 3: [Mascot Naming](https://codeforces.com/contest/2068/problem/F)
